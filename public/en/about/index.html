About · Moses Zegbah Zimay

Accountant and full-stack developer.

I keep the books straight for an international coffee organization and a global church network — and I build the web tools those teams need too.

Moses Zegbah Zimay (Moses Zimay) is an accounting assistant based in Abidjan, Côte d'Ivoire (Ivory Coast), focused on audit, internal control and fraud prevention, and building a career in forensic accounting and financial fraud investigation. Since 2022 he has worked in the Finance Department of the Inter-African Coffee Organization (IACO), a 25-member-country coffee producers' organization: multi-currency bank reconciliations (XOF, EUR), preparing the annual statutory audit (MNG Audit & Consulting) and the external audit of a GIZ-funded project (BDO), and bookkeeping on SAGE 100. He has 7+ years of finance and accounting experience (SAGE 100, QuickBooks, Xero, SYCEBNL) and has trained in cybersecurity (penetration testing, brute-force attacks). He is also a full-stack web developer (Django, WordPress/WooCommerce, Shopify). Bilingual in French and English.

Goal

Becoming a forensic accountant and financial fraud investigation expert.

What already prepares me for it, day to day:

  • Certified external audits: prepared IACO's audits by MNG Audit & Consulting and the GIZ project audit by BDO — supporting evidence, auditor requests, accounts certified true and compliant.
  • Monthly multi-account, multi-currency bank reconciliations (XOF, EUR) — the first line of defence for spotting anomalies and misappropriation.
  • Physical stock counts and fixed-asset verification with formal reports — matching what is recorded to what actually exists.
  • Monitoring compliance with financial rules and procedures, cash and per-diem handling, and a full paper trail for every transaction.
  • Cybersecurity training (penetration testing, brute-force attacks) to understand digital fraud and how access is protected.

My story

Numbers and code.

Je suis Assistant Comptable à l'Organisation Interafricaine du Café — rapprochements bancaires, préparation des audits, clôture des comptes. Sept ans de ce travail m'ont appris une chose qui est restée : les chiffres n'ont de sens que si le processus derrière eux est irréprochable.

En parallèle, je construis des applications web full-stack et j'automatise les systèmes sur lesquels tournent les petites entreprises — sites web, boutiques WordPress et Shopify, tableaux de bord financiers. Deux disciplines, une seule exigence : rien ne sort tant que ce n'est pas juste.
