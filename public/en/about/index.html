About · Moses Zegbah Zimay

Accountant and full-stack developer.

I keep the books straight for an international coffee organization and a global church network — and I build the web tools those teams need too.

Moses Zegbah Zimay (Moses Zimay) is an accounting assistant based in Abidjan, Côte d'Ivoire (Ivory Coast), focused on audit, internal control and fraud prevention, and building a career in forensic accounting and financial fraud investigation. Since 2022 he has worked in the Finance Department of the Inter-African Coffee Organization (IACO), a 25-member-country coffee producers' organization: multi-currency bank reconciliations (XOF, EUR), preparing the annual statutory audit (MNG Audit & Consulting) and the external audit of a GIZ-funded project (BDO), and bookkeeping on SAGE 100. He has 7+ years of finance and accounting experience (SAGE 100, QuickBooks, Xero, SYCEBNL), and has trained in open-source intelligence (OSINT) with the Basel Institute on Governance (ICAR) and in cybersecurity (penetration testing, brute-force attacks). He is also a full-stack web developer (Django, WordPress/WooCommerce, Shopify). Bilingual in French and English.

Goal

Becoming a forensic accountant and financial fraud investigation expert.

What already prepares me for it, day to day:

  • Certified external audits: prepared IACO's audits by MNG Audit & Consulting and the GIZ project audit by BDO — supporting evidence, auditor requests, accounts certified true and compliant.
  • Monthly multi-account, multi-currency bank reconciliations (XOF, EUR) — the first line of defence for spotting anomalies and misappropriation.
  • Physical stock counts and fixed-asset verification with formal reports — matching what is recorded to what actually exists.
  • Monitoring compliance with financial rules and procedures, cash and per-diem handling, and a full paper trail for every transaction.
  • Open-source intelligence (OSINT) training with the International Centre for Asset Recovery of the Basel Institute on Governance — the investigative method used to trace financial flows and recover stolen assets.
  • Cybersecurity training (penetration testing, brute-force attacks) to understand digital fraud and how access is protected.

My story

Numbers and code.

I'm an Accounting Assistant at the Inter-African Coffee Organization — bank reconciliations, audit preparation, closing the books. Seven years of this work taught me one thing that stuck: numbers only mean something if the process behind them is airtight.

Alongside that, I build full-stack web applications and automate the systems small businesses run on — websites, WordPress and Shopify stores, financial dashboards. Two disciplines, one standard: nothing goes out until it's right.
