Privacy policy

Last updated 23 October 2026.

Who is responsible

This website, zimaymoses.com, is published by Moses Zegbah Zimay, Abidjan, Côte d'Ivoire. Contact: zimaymoses0@gmail.com.

What is recorded when you visit

For each page you open, the site records: your IP address; the approximate location derived from it (country, region, city); the page visited; the page or site that sent you here (for example Google, LinkedIn or ChatGPT); your browser, operating system and device type, as reported by your browser; your browser's language; and the date and time.

The site uses no cookies, no advertising trackers and no third-party analytics. No form on the site collects personal information.

Why

  • Security: detecting attempts to break into the site, such as scans for admin pages.
  • Audience: knowing which pages are read and how visitors find the site, so it can be improved.

The legal basis is the publisher's legitimate interest in securing and improving the site.

Who processes the data

The site is hosted by Vercel Inc., which provides the location estimate. Visit records are stored temporarily with Upstash Inc. (database service), then on the publisher's own computer. These providers may store data outside Côte d'Ivoire. The data is never sold, shared with advertisers or used to identify you personally.

How long

Visit records are kept for a maximum of 12 months, then deleted automatically.

Your rights

Under Ivorian law No. 2013-450 on the protection of personal data and, where it applies, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you can ask to access, correct or delete the data concerning you, or object to its processing. Write to zimaymoses0@gmail.com, stating your IP address and the approximate date of your visit. You may also lodge a complaint with the ARTCI (Côte d'Ivoire) or your local data protection authority.
